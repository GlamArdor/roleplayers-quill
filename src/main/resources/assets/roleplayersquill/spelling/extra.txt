# Words no dictionary of a real language holds, and every roleplayer writes anyway.
#
# Two kinds of thing are in here. The first is the vocabulary a fantasy book is made of – races,
# creatures, the half-dozen words a tabletop borrowed from English and never gave back – which no
# Russian word list will ever carry and which would otherwise be underlined on every page. The
# second is the setting of the Reign server, whose wiki was read for it: its realms, its houses, its
# peoples and its gods.
#
# One word to a line, in lower case, hashes for comments. Words beginning with a capital are left
# alone anyway unless that setting is turned off, so most of what this buys is the lower-case forms:
# "дварфы", "аш'каары", "квента".
#
# A word of your own goes in config/roleplayersquill/dictionary.txt, not here.
